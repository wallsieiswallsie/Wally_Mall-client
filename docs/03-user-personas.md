# User proto-personas

[Business context](01-business-context.md) · [Flows](04-user-flows.md)

**Proto-personas based on current product assumptions.** These are design hypotheses, not interviewed participants, demographic findings, or evidence of actual adoption. Current behavior below is hypothesized and should be checked in research.

## Local Buyer

- **Goals:** find an item available locally, inspect its price and condition, identify the seller.
- **Pain points:** scattered listings, unfamiliar merchant names, uncertain availability.
- **Current behavior hypothesis:** search social posts, ask contacts, or visit stores.
- **Wally Mall value:** product/category/store discovery in one catalog.
- **Key journey:** Home → search/category → product → store or contact preview; save an item for the current session.

## UMKM / Home Seller

- **Goals:** make offerings discoverable outside an existing audience with little setup effort.
- **Pain points:** repeated product questions, upkeep burden, concern about exposing a home address.
- **Current behavior hypothesis:** share products through messaging and social channels and handle interest directly.
- **Wally Mall value:** structured listing fields and a storefront; any demand benefit remains unvalidated.
- **Key journey:** Buka Lapak → five-step onboarding → dashboard preview → product form → preview save.

## Established Local Store

- **Goals:** expose useful catalog information and route interest to a responsive seller.
- **Pain points:** maintaining availability across channels and understanding which listings attract interest.
- **Current behavior hypothesis:** combine physical retail with informal online discovery.
- **Wally Mall value:** searchable products and store context; future measurement could inform listing upkeep.
- **Key journey:** same seller preview as the home seller; there is no separate business account tier, staff access, bulk import, or branch management.

No marketplace-administrator persona is included because the app contains no admin flow. If moderation becomes agreed scope, define its user needs and operating process before adding that interface.
